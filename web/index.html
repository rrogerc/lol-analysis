Ranked builds — theoretical damage model

First-principles simulation: exact stat sheets, ability formulas, item procs, expected-value crit — no match data. Pick a scenario; every item-pool combination is simulated against that stat dummy and ranked by expected kill time. That differs from raw kill time only for builds carrying an execute: crit here is an expected value, so the health curve is fixed and an execute window always catches the target — real crit would land in it only some of the time, so the two timelines are blended by that probability. Ties on the same attack are broken by how much damage the build had to spare. Click a build to see where its damage comes from.

Item pool

Every ranked build is one boots choice plus five of these. Only items whose damage passives the engine models faithfully are enumerated — anything else would be ranked on raw stats alone and land too low. In-game "Limited to 1" groups (Last Whisper, Hydra, Tear, Spellblade, Energized, Lifeline, Annul) are enforced — no build carries two of a group.
