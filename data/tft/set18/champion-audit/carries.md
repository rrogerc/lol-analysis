# Full carries driver audit — pinned 18.1d

This audit covers **15 champions / 16 champion-form entries**, including Gromp AD and AP and Kha'Zix. It reuses the completed AoE audit and adds damage, form statistics, mana/casts/AS, passives, timing, procs and utility. No production sources changed; no enumeration, rebuild, warm or publication was run.

**Evidence labels:** `verified` means agreement with pinned source/calculation wiring or a mathematical invariant; it does not mean verified in the live game. `discrepancy` identifies observed behavior conflicting with the pinned text or internally inconsistent arithmetic. `omitted` is an explicitly absent feature. `assumed` is a chosen model behavior. `needs-runtime-evidence` is unresolved and must not be presented as a proven bug. Priorities include investigation priority, not only defect severity.

This driver review used the repository's saved 18.1d data. Tooltips, source locations, findings, test coverage and missing checks are in [findings.json](findings.json), under `groups.carries` (16 entries). Exact coefficients and legal-star kits remain in the hash-pinned source snapshot. Damage formulas below use **a = current AD / star-scaled base AD**, **p = AP / 100**. The AD coefficients already contain star scaling; treating them as ordinary AD percentages would double-count it.

Engine `75b1dc3aed55577eb967d10f3ea9904790ab01f61cbf917be54ef1e01f0b9b45`; snapshot input `1c7e857debea22df4f16936771bd1ea07800f59bdbd3f05a0574434c9e18067c`.

**Common boundaries:** role mana is 7/attack + 2/sec for Casters and 10/attack for Marksmen/Assassins. The engine assumes generic cast/mana locks and binary clump/spread coverage; attack windups and missile travel are not resolved from target positions. Item/trait proc correctness and board-wide utility are consolidated in the parent shared-engine/trait audit. A golden replay only proves compatibility with prior outputs.

New focused evidence: Kha'Zix receives only 10 isolation mana with Adaptive Helm rather than 11.5 from its all-sources multiplier; Pebbles' complete 2.857s channel pays only 2.75s of constant-rate damage; Draven's crit-enabled ticking bleed and immediate remaining-damage cashout disagree. Xayah's feather mana/crit rules remain a high-priority runtime question, not a proven defect.

The table highlights decisions; the JSON covers all remaining timing/passive/utility domains. Additional omissions include Gromp AD's 30% Slow, Karma's Slow, Lux's shared-trait mana and unsupported Avatar choice, leap/dash/blink movement, and LeBlanc's post-combat copying economy. Soraka's mark is correctly persistent because her wording says a star **previously** fell on the target; Cassiopeia's permanent poison mark is different because expired poison should no longer make an enemy currently poisoned. Tristana's damage pool is correctly divided among targets, and her transfer-on-death rule is explicit.

Gromp AD growth was inspected carefully: `ad_extra += .3` is a **fractional** +30% base-AD gain because Sheet.ad multiplies that term by base AD (`fight.rs:122–123,889–890`). It is not a +0.3-flat-AD bug. Gromp's AP and AD forms use different mana bars (0/45 versus 20/80) and star-scaled attack values (45 versus 67.5 at two stars); the form kit correctly supplies them.

Verification performed: 11 selected existing tests passed (formula folding, Adaptor forms, Kha'Zix isolation presence, curve overrides, starting mana, cast animation, DoT crits/fractional ticks). Six additional controlled native cases are saved in `observations.json (groups.carries)`; the previous 13 targeting cases are reused from `observations.json (groups.carries-aoe)`. The Draven 120%-chance probe is an extrapolation concern only: no claim is made that ordinary finite winners reach it. No live-game capture or newly installed game script was used.

Most useful missing oracles: (1) preserve spell targets through primary death; (2) exact full/partial channel integration; (3) mana from each source with/without multipliers; (4) damage conservation when a DoT is consumed; (5) Xayah empowered attack mana, normal crits and animation; (6) Ezreal's fourth-cast replacement/addition semantics; (7) actual projectile, bounce, star and dash timestamps. Correcting these should follow source/runtime evidence instead of introducing damage bonuses or role penalties.
