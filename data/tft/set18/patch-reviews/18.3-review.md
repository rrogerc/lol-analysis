# Review supporting the 18.3 patch manifest

Reviewed 2026-09-23 against the published 18.2b snapshot. Riot published the
[18.3 notes](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/teamfight-tactics-patch-18-3/)
on September 22; the scheduled refresh first saw them at 18:41 UTC that day and stopped on every run since
(four runs, the last at 2026-09-23 12:42 UTC) with

    Blossom Charms Animate Shop Duration: trait change needs an explicit reviewed field mapping

That is the updater working as designed, not a defect: Riot files the Blossom-empowered Wisps under TRAITS, so
`tft_update._discover` finds the Blossom trait by name and refuses to guess a row. This review answers that line
and every other one.

What changed upstream, against the published 18.2b snapshot:

- Riot's article: 75 numeric lines and 142 bullets, no dated mid-patch update yet (so the label is `18.3`).
- The MetaTFT lookup is byte-identical (generated 2026-09-11, still stamped PBE). It holds the 18.2 values of
  every line below, so each change is an override on top of it.
- The 79 character bins are identical, now served from the live 16.19 client.
- CommunityDragon's export moved to the live 16.19 client (`16.19.8207193+branch.releases-16-19`,
  Last-Modified 2026-09-23 06:05 GMT). What it does and does not show is below; it matters for Invoker.

Every one of these reaches a fight through the row except four that no fight reads and are mapped only so the
archive says what the game says (as the 18.2b review did for LeBlanc's clone chance): Coven's essence (Coven is
unmodeled), both Ornn quest rows (artifact anvils are post-combat economy) and Solar Lux's bonus (Lux fights as
her base record). Points a reader might trip on:

- **Nidalee's armor penetration is a real change to the model.** The entry's other bullet says the game applied
  60% on 18.2 while the row said 40%. The driver reads the row, so the model swiped with 40% through 18.2b; it now
  uses 60%, which is what the game did then and does now ("no functional change" is from the game's side).
- **Coven column 2.** The lookup's row has no column-2 breakpoint (`[[1, 18], [3, 32], [4, 60]]`), so column 2 read
  18 where Riot's old value and both CommunityDragon exports say 25. All four columns are written.
- **Gromp's AD form.** `tft.kit_spec` takes an Adaptor form's base attack damage from its own `AutoAttackDamage`
  row, so the stat alone would not have reached a fight. The whole row scales by 50/45, the rule `tft_update`
  applies when a lookup's base attack damage moves; star 4 (168.75) is that derivation, stated by no source and
  enumerated by nothing. The mapping needed a `siblingRowAcknowledgement`: the wrong-row guard spreads the note's
  single 45 over four stars, finds the per-star row 70% away, and flags the 30%-slow rows (30 at every star) as
  closer. A base attack-damage line cannot mean a slow.
- Riot's three-value lines leave the 4-star value of the lookup in place (Alistar, Azir, Cassiopeia, Murkwolf,
  Rammus, Warwick, Gromp's DoT), so a few rows now read lower at 4 stars than at 3 (Rammus 725 then 650). Nothing
  enumerates 4 stars (`STARS_BY_COST` stops at 3; 4-5 costs at 2).

## Evidence, and where it disagrees

Riot's notes are the primary statement. Two other sources were read:

**TFTraits** (third-party; every champion and trait page saved 2026-09-23 15:32 UTC). It shows every value mapped
above that it displays, with one exception: Cassiopeia 420/630/1020, 420 at 1 star where Riot says 425. The note
is applied. But its agreement is weaker evidence than in the 18.2b review: on its 18.3 pages Ashe (trail 4 s,
5/8 AD), Camille (160/240/410%), LeBlanc (40% clone chance) and Teemo (60/90/135) are back at their values from
*before* 18.2's September 14 update, which it showed correctly on 2026-09-20. No Riot note reverts them, so the
snapshot keeps the September 14 values. It looks as though TFTraits re-derived 18.3 from older base data plus
Riot's 18.3 notes, in which case its agreement confirms this review's reading of the notes rather than the game.
Its starting-mana field is not evidence either: it shows 0 for 32 units the snapshot starts above 0, where
CommunityDragon agrees with the snapshot (and 30 for Ornn, whom both give 40).

**CommunityDragon's 16.19 export** (the live client's files; also `map22.bin`, read directly for Invoker):

- Set 18 champion abilities carry no values in it (the curve tables are not exported), so it cannot check a unit
  line. Ornn's quest text now reads "increased by @DamageTaken3StarScalar@ at 3-star", consistent with the note.
- Its trait constants still hold the 18.2 values for Defender (120), Hunter (45/65%), Inferno (3.5/4.5) and Coven
  (18/25/32/60): the client's files lag the server on this patch.
- **Invoker is the exception, and it disagrees with Riot.** `DA_18_Invoker` `{de27cb95}`, the variable the
  tooltip prints as `@InvokerManaBonus@` and which held 3/4/6/9 in the 16.18 export, is now **2/3/5/8**; Riot's note
  says 3/4/6/9 ⇒ 3/4/6/**8** and TFTraits shows 3/4/6/8. The note is applied (primary, corroborated, and the same
  export lags the server elsewhere on this very patch). If the client is right, an Invoker regenerates one mana a
  second less than modeled at 2-4 Invokers. Open.
- Base stats: Brambleback's attack damage (120) and Rengar's attack speed (0.75) caught up with Riot's 18.2 notes,
  retiring two 18.2b disagreements. One is new, below.

## Also corrected with this publication: Team Planner IDs

Not a balance line, but found while checking 18.3's client data. Riot renumbered two champions' Team Planner IDs
in the 16.18 client (live 18.2), and 16.19 keeps them: Ivern `405` → `404`, Lux `413` → `405` (hexadecimal).
`team-planner.json` had been pinned from the 16.17 export on 2026-09-07, so from 18.2 until this publication
"Copy to TFT" exported Ivern as Lux and Lux as an ID no champion uses. The catalog is now pinned to the 16.19
export (`sha256 0cdb14f5e271…`, identical to `latest`); every other champion's ID, name and cost is unchanged.
MetaTFT's lookup (a PBE build) already had the new IDs; it is still not used as a fallback. The catalog is
presentation data, so it changed the site bundle and no calculation.

06:05 GMT export), matched these bindings, reconciled to the result above, and published at 12:20:53: 1,770
champion scenarios and all 8 composition contexts recalculated, 2 h 23 min end to end, the champion cells
taking about 2.5 min. The dashboard restarted itself at 12:21:03 through `lol-dashboard-reload.path`.

and the leaderboards) reports 18.3, with Ivern `404` and Lux `405` in the planner catalog. The archived

This publication also carries every model change committed since the last one (2026-09-22 06:42 MDT): the
per-ability radii in fights and on the composition board, timed Sunder/Shred per target, Solar's true share,
Azir's Summoner row and positions in the composition team measurement. They, not 18.3, account for most of the
composition board's movement. In c2-clump-mixed Gromp carried 163 of 208 kept boards before and 27 now, with
Warwick, buffed by 18.3, taking over (123). In c3-clump-mixed Kog'Maw fell from 34 to 12 and Mama Beak from 130 to

leaderboard (best stars, clumped, no traits) Cassiopeia moved #20 → #16, Nidalee #11 → #9 (her AD form now
builds Infinity Edge with 60% armor ignored), Gromp #22 → #21, Azir kept #23 with a faster clear
(21.75 → 20.92 s), and Veigar now clears inside 30 s. Among tanks Alistar moved #9 → #7, and Rammus held
34 → 44 s at high traits. The trait nerfs show where they should: Hunters (Caitlyn, Tristana, Cinderling)
lose places on high-trait boards and Defenders hold a little less.
