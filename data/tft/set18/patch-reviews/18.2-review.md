# Review supporting the 18.2 patch manifest

The manifest binds all decisions to the exact freshly parsed Riot article, candidate lookup and bins, and the previous audit. Numeric corrections apply only to their explicit target and listed stars/columns; they do not loosen continuity checks for any future patch.

Source: [Riot 18.2 patch notes](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/teamfight-tactics-patch-18-2/). Reviewed 2026-09-10.

Validation: 98 target mappings; 226 exact dispositions; all 158 numeric entries and 246 article entries covered. Every previous value and proposed normalized value passes. The updater’s bound-review loader and full in-memory reconciliation pass, producing 153 current audit checks. Six obsolete Wit’s End damage checks are retained as historical evidence because its new stage encoding is unresolved and the item is excluded from scored builds. Patch 18.2 was subsequently rebuilt, published and verified on September 10; see the final publication record below. The component reviews describe their own narrower checks.

The component reviews left 28 article entries outside their slices: 23 cosmetic availability/catalog leaves, three XP purchase-cost changes, and two three-star unit headings. Each is explicitly reviewed in the manifest. XP cost changes do not alter the supplied fixed-level roster or champion-copy budget. Cosmetic names do not add champions to the roster. The headings retain their numeric descendants’ three-star scope.

Forty-six redundant numeric-note markers were omitted after checking each parsed numerical statement had an explicit mapping or disposition. Overlapping champion/trait bug reviews retain the champion’s specific classification and both substantive reasons; the Murkwolf handoff is resolved by its explicit geometry limitation.

### Mechanics and bug dispositions

- **Veigar — numeric-model-correction-required:** See supplemental Veigar correction; do not ignore this as a harmless tooltip note when the driver reads its stale row.
- **Sentinel — retained-abstract-geometry-with-explicit-limitation:** Largest-line targeting may now exclude the current target. In supported all-clump/isolated-spread extremes the selected hit counts need not change, but real mixed layouts remain unmodeled. Do not claim the new targeting algorithm is implemented or add an arbitrary damage multiplier.
- **Ashe — bug-not-reproduced-by-abstract-model:** Driver a.rs:Ashe::cast applies deterministic clump/all or spread/one line coverage; it never simulated random wrong-line casts. Preserve explicit numeric arrow buff; no invented accuracy bonus. Actual line choice remains an approximation.

- **Yorick — presentation-only:** Spirit Walker naming replaces a tooltip Ghoul inconsistency; no numeric, timing or recipient change is specified. Internal legacy row names GhoulHealthBase/GhoulPercentHealth do not rename the game entity.
- **Draven — outside-existing-economy-model:** Bounty Seeker quest generation/level10 XP rewards are not simulated by the combat driver.
- **All champions — presentation-only:** Shop-purchased two-star highlighting/size is not a stats or star-scaling change.
