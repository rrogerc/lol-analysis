# Review supporting the 18.2b patch manifest

Reviewed 2026-09-20 against the published 18.2 snapshot. Two things changed upstream
since that snapshot was published on September 10, and this review covers both:

1. Riot added a dated mid-patch update to the [18.2 notes](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/teamfight-tactics-patch-18-2/):
   `<h2>MID-PATCH UPDATE</h2>` / `<h4>SEPTEMBER 14</h4>`, 14 numeric lines and 14 other bullets, plus a
   sixteen-bullet "18.2 PERFORMANCE/STABILITY IMPROVEMENTS" block.
2. MetaTFT regenerated its lookup on 2026-09-11 (`coreHash ccee3f1e…`; the published snapshot runs on the
   pre-launch build of 2026-08-16). 46 of 81 unit definitions, 13 of 36 traits and 28 items differ; one excluded
   artifact (`DA_Artifact_DeathsDefiance`) is gone. No pool item's effective value changes.

The CommunityDragon export and the 79 character bins are identical to the published snapshot's.

## Why this is 18.2b and why the refresh was stuck

Every scheduled refresh since September 10 stopped with exit 2 and
`XP From Level 8-9: no unique existing champion or reviewed target mapping` (the last success is 2026-09-10
17:26 UTC; the state file's ten retained runs all carry this message). Two defects met:

- `tft_update._outside` exempted the player-XP table only in the main article's shape (`SYSTEMS` /
  `XP PER LEVEL`, "Level 8 to Level 9"). The update files it as `MID-PATCH UPDATE` / `SYSTEMS`,
  "XP From Level 8-9". Both shapes are now exempt, and only a line that *is* a table row under a systems heading.
- `tft.PatchNotesParser` knew 18.1's hotfix shape (`<h2>Mid-Patch Updates</h2>` with one `<h3>` per date).
  18.2's singular heading with the date as an `<h4>` left `updates` empty, so the candidate kept the label
  `18.2`. A same-label candidate never loads a review file (`_BoundReview` loads
  `patch-reviews/<patch>.json` only when the patch label changes, so that an unchanged scheduled run cannot
  reapply an old review), which means no review could have unblocked it. The parser now recognises the shape:
  the candidate is `18.2b`, `updates = ["SEPTEMBER 14"]`, and this file's manifest is the one that loads.
  Riot's 18.1 page parses to the identical document before and after the change.

## What the regenerated lookup changed without a patch note

The updater already lets a lookup catch up with a value the audit holds. 94 of the 98 values the 18.2 review
mapped are now in the lookup itself, the corrected Nidalee row among them, so those needed nothing; 19 stat
overrides retire for the same reason. What is left is every coordinate where the new lookup says something the
published snapshot did not: 74 records, 165 coordinates (142 on units, 23 on traits).
They are `lookupChanges` records: the published value, the lookup's value, a decision and the evidence, all
checked against the staged files when the manifest loads.

Evidence, in order of weight: Riot's notes (primary); the archived CommunityDragon export (an independent
extraction of the game files: base stats and trait effect variables, the latter under hashed names matched by
value); TFTraits' champion and trait pages saved on 2026-09-20 (third-party; whether it shares a data source
with MetaTFT is unknown, so agreement shows the lookup is no outlier rather than proving it); the LoL wiki's
Set 18 pages (community-edited and unevenly maintained: Nidalee, Pebbles, Lux and Elder Dragon are current,
while Kha'Zix, Master Yi, Brambleback, Ahri and LeBlanc still show launch values that Riot's own 18.2 notes
contradict, so a stale wiki page is not counted against the lookup).

106 of the 165 coordinates are stated by at least one source besides the lookup. Wherever such a source
exists it agrees with the staged lookup, and never with the published value. On that base rate the other
59 are accepted on the lookup's word alone, and their records say so: 42 are 4-star values, which nothing
else publishes and no scenario enumerates (`STARS_BY_COST` stops at 3; the single exception is Pebbles'
615, which the wiki does state, and that record's evidence says so). The remaining 17 are the non-4-star
coordinates of the 23 records that are lookup-only as a whole — tooltip, quest, sacrifice and
unmodeled-summon rows that no fight reads — which their `reason` and the table below mark "thin".

One value is **kept against the lookup**: Brambleback's 3-star `FrenzyArmorIgnoreFlat` stays 0.1. The lookup
writes the 1-2 star base at every star, but Riot's 18.2 line "3-star 4-costs Brambleback Armor Ignore: 10 + 50%
AP ⇒ 10 + 70% AP" states a 10% base, and a primary statement outranks the lookup.

4-star attack damage: the published `AutoAttackDamage` star-4 values (Brambleback 405, Kha'Zix 135, Master Yi
202.5) were this repository's own base × 1.5³, never a source's. The lookup now lists 3 × base for them and
3.375 × for others, so it is game data and is accepted; stars 1-3 keep the reviewed base × 1.5ⁿ. `damageByStar`
is accepted only as a verbatim copy of that row.

## Known gaps this review leaves open

- **Azir's Summoner bonus is overstated in trait contexts.** Live, Summoner gives Azir +20% (+30% at 3 units)
  through the new `AzirDamageMult` row; `trait-effects.json` still feeds `DamageMult` (+45%/+67.5%) to every
  summoner and `late.rs` multiplies Azir's soldiers by it. His bare cells, and so the default leaderboard, do
  not use the trait. Needs `trait-effects.json` and the engine, which this review does not touch.
  **Fixed 2026-09-22:** the mapping gained an optional `azirDamageMult` and his driver reads it, falling back to
  `DamageMult` on patches without the row. The manifest's records above keep the review's original wording.
- **Diana's mana** (lookup and TFTraits 40, CommunityDragon 30) and **Ashe's, Draven's and Lux's range**
  (5 against 6) are unresolved source conflicts. Range has no modeled effect; Diana's mana would.
- **Lux** fights as her base record (40 armor/MR); every origin variant has 45 in both sources.
- The 54 coordinates accepted on the lookup's word alone (37 of them 4-star values).
- A same-label revision (Riot editing the SEPTEMBER 14 block in place, or MetaTFT regenerating again) still has
  no review path: it would need a new mechanism, not this file.

## Validation

- `tft_update.reconcile(candidate, previous, notes)` passes with this manifest; without it the same inputs stop
  at "Leblanc Clone Chance: qualified or unknown ability label requires an explicit audit mapping". The result
  has 122 current checks against 18.2's 153: 40 item checks move to `outOfScopeChecks` (their excluded
  definitions changed), the three `AutoAttackDamage` checks whose 4-star entry the lookup rewrites split into
  stars 1-3 and star 4, and 12 checks are new. It records 7 applied changes, 74 accepted lookup changes, 19
  retired stat corrections, 9 explained source disagreements and none inherited.
- Live, inside a worktree, stopping before the warm: `tft.cmd_fetch(SimpleNamespace(set=18, patch=None,
  force=True), automatic=True, prepare=...)` downloaded Riot's page, the lookup, the export and 79 bins, staged
  `18.2b`, matched these bindings, reconciled, passed `check_patch_notes` (122 of 122 current) and
  `check_sources` (65 records compared, 9 disagreements, all explained, none unreviewed), and reached
  `prepare`. The live overrides equal the offline reconciliation. Re-run against upstream on 2026-09-20 at
  20:40 MDT with the same result: no source has moved since these bindings were taken.
- Reconciled overrides against the published ones: 128 pinned coordinates fall to 84. All 19 unit stat
  corrections and all 30 excluded-item corrections retire. Five rows are new (Ashe `RiftDuration` and
  `RiftDamagePerSecond`, Camille `AbilityDamage`, LeBlanc `PercentChanceToPrint`, Teemo `APDamage`); Maokai's
  pinned mana moves 90 → 100 and Solar's `PercentIncreasePer3Star` gains its inactive column; Brambleback's two
  armor-ignore rows change at stars 1-2 (the flat row's 4-star entry as well); in 13 further pinned rows only
  the 4-star entry moves to the lookup's value, and Cassiopeia's `Damage` gains a 4-star entry it did not have.
  No publication was made and no fight was run by this review.
